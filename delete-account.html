Delete your Fullo account

  1. Open Fullo and go to Profile → Data & privacy.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your profile, diary, weigh-ins, workouts, recipes, products, Health data and rewards are deleted immediately. Encrypted backups roll over within 35 days. Subscription records required by tax law may be kept by the App Store or Google Play.

Your subscription is not cancelled automatically — cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
